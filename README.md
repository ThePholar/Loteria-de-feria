# Privacy Policy — Lotería de Feria

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026  
**Application:** Lotería de Feria (`com.thepholar.loteriadeferia`)  
**Developer:** ThePholar

> **Offline-First & 100% Private:** Lotería de Feria is designed to be enjoyed without accounts, registrations, ads, or activity tracking.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

**Lotería de Feria does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.**

- No user accounts, logins, names, phone numbers, or email addresses are required.
- No advertisements, trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, or behavioral profiling are included.
- We do not operate external servers that receive information about your games or your device.

## 2. Internet Access (Google Play Purchases & License Verification)

The application works offline. Network access is used **strictly and exclusively** to communicate with official **Google Play Billing** services for the following purposes:

- Retrieving local pricing and availability for the PRO subscription (monthly plan with a 1-month free trial and annual plan) and the Lifetime PRO one-time purchase.
- Processing, verifying, and restoring active licenses or subscriptions linked to your Google Play account.

All payments and billing are handled directly by Google Play. The developer **never** has access to your credit card details, bank information, or payment credentials. Payment processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Local On-Device Storage

All data generated while using the app remains **exclusively within your device's private local storage**, including:

- Language, voice, speed, narration style, and visual theme preferences.
- Favorite boards and Trophy Room progress.
- Cached PRO license status so you can continue using the app 100% offline.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you clear app data or uninstall the application.

## 4. Crash Reports & Technical Support (Strictly Voluntary)

If an unexpected crash or technical error occurs, the app saves a temporary diagnostic log locally on your device containing basic technical details (device brand, model, Android version, app version, and error stack trace).

- **This diagnostic log is never transmitted automatically.**
- It is only shared if you manually choose to send it via **Email**, **Telegram**, or **Signal** for technical support. Any report you voluntarily send is used solely to diagnose and fix the reported issue.

## 5. Local Device Features

- **Voice (Text-to-Speech):** Uses your Android device's local Text-to-Speech engine.
- **PDF Export & Printing:** Board and card deck PDFs are generated locally on your device and are only shared or printed when triggered by you via the Android system sheet.
- **Vibration & Keep Screen Awake:** Used locally during gameplay according to your preferences.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Lotería de Feria is a traditional family-friendly game. Because the application does not collect any personal or identifiable information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect app improvements or Google Play policy requirements. Any updates will be published on this page and inside the app with the revised date.
